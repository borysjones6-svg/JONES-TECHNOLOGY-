NEED MORE INFORMATION?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about working with Jones Technology.

What services does Jones Technology offer?

We offer website design, landing pages, website maintenance, WhatsApp integration, digital design and custom web solutions.

How much does a website cost?

Our indicative website development prices depend on the type of project and required features. Visit our pricing page for package ranges and request a custom quotation.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from businesses and individuals in Kenya and other countries. Project requirements and communication arrangements can be discussed remotely.

Does the website price include a domain?

Domain registration and paid hosting are separate costs unless a quotation specifically states otherwise. We can discuss suitable options for your project.

How do I request a website?

Complete the enquiry form on this website or contact us directly through WhatsApp. Tell us about your business and what you want to build.

Can you customise a website for my business?

Yes. We can discuss your preferred design, content, functionality and business requirements before preparing a suitable quotation.

How can I join Jones Technology?

Visit our Join Our Team section and complete the application form. Opportunities depend on our requirements and available openings.
